Concept proposal for the current walk-in houses. ## Experience

Enter the real house and pass through a short vestibule into a larger matching interior. Carry the exterior's materials, door detailing and daylight into this transition so it feels like another part of the same home. Keep the exit visible and recognizable when looking back.

The vestibule contains an empty recessed achievement wall and room for a future shelf/display. Reserve this space now; no achievement system or trophy content is proposed for this pass. It sits beside the route, never across the expansion end of the hall.

Each collection room holds three independent pedestal positions on the left and three on the right, with a generous straight aisle between them. Low freestanding bases make each piggy feel collected and displayed. The collection plate faces the aisle. Piggies remain the focal point; decorations stay on the walls or above camera height.

A broad doorway at the far end frames the next room. While locked, its themed closed doors carry a large readable padlock and the required rebirth count. When unlocked, the doors clear the opening so the extension feels continuous. Use the game's configured requirement in the eventual implementation; the concept's lock label intentionally supplies no invented rebirth number.

## Modular construction proposal

`House entrance -> Achievement vestibule -> Room 01 -> Gate -> Room 02 -> Gate -> ...`

- **Room shell:** floor, walls, ceiling and decorative finish; no pedestals merged into it.
- **Six slot anchors:** three per side, with identical positions across themes. Each accepts a separate pedestal model. Remove the entire pedestal and its collection plate for an unavailable slot, leaving a finished floor rather than a hole.
- **Pedestal:** independent base, piggy anchor, collection plate and label attachment. Room access and individual pedestal unlocks remain separate concepts.
- **Gate:** its own frame, door panels, collision and lock-label attachment. Reserve its operating clearance so it cannot swing through a piggy. Final hinge/retraction geometry needs a blockout check.
- **Connections:** matching entrance/exit markers, floor height and orientation. Append another room at the previous room's exit marker. Keep the closing gate/end cap separate so extending the hall does not require rebuilding its art.
- **Theme kit:** replace shell surfaces, structural ribs, door design, fixture shapes and pedestal dressing together while retaining room connections and slot anchors.
- **Achievement space:** one dedicated entrance alcove with a display mount and clear wall, independent of the repeating room.

Start the blockout near the existing hall's 38-stud clear width and 16-stud central lane. These are starting proportions, not approved measurements. Fit three stations per side, then check avatar/camera clearance, collection reach, gate motion and views into the next room in Studio before setting the module length. Every theme keeps the same useful floor area; ceiling silhouettes and decorative geometry can vary outside that clearance.
