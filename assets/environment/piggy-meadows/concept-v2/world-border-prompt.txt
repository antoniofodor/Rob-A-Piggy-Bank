Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a coherent art-direction concept board redesigning the ENTIRE WORLD BORDER of an existing Roblox game, Rob A Piggy Bank. Reference images 1 and 2 show the current game and its problem: enormous overlapping rectangular grass-capped wall slabs, poorly joined corners, repeated stair-step blocks, ugly outer structures and flat outer shelves. These images are spatial/layout references only: REPLACE that border language, do not reproduce the bad slabs. Preserve the central straight road, two rows of player lots/houses, back-of-lot piggy roaming meadows, and tunnel road entrances at opposite ends.
User correction: all assets and environment must be noticeably LOWER POLY than polished illustrated nature scenes. Deliberately simple large flat-shaded facets, very few planes per boulder, low-sided tree crowns in 3-5 masses, large solid-color surfaces, no individually detailed leaves, no photoreal rock texture, no tiny cracks, no high-detail moss, no fine grass carpet. Friendly clean Roblox toy-world quality, not ugly cubes or a generic minecraft world. The improvement comes from good silhouettes, spacing and connected geometry.
Board title "WORLD BORDER — LOW-POLY CONCEPT". Landscape 16:9, clean small typography, pale cream background behind detail panels.
TOP 65%: large elevated three-quarter masterplan render showing all four sides and corners of the game enclosed in a coherent irregular rounded-rectangle valley. Make the central playable village large and legible, not a tiny village buried inside giant mountains. Border occupies sensible scale.
New border is THREE clear landscape layers, innermost to outermost:
A. "1  CONNECTED CLIFF": low-to-medium irregular continuous faceted rock escarpment outside the wandering lanes, a shared silhouette and continuous grass top. Several broad rock faces per segment, sloped buttress feet, clean grass-rock boundary, no separate overhanging rectangular lids. Height changes happen gradually in broad rhythms. Each corner bends naturally through 2-3 angled facets rather than one wall intersecting another. No scattered floating cap plates, no visible intersecting blocks, no thin shelves slicing into adjacent faces, no repeating tall-short battlements.
B. "2  GRASSY SLOPES": broad gently rolling low-poly hills rising and falling behind that cliff, with sparse chunky trees in intentional groups. These are connecting terrain masses, not a second hard vertical wall or flat rectangular apron. Ground continues between layers, no visible void, gaps or floating island underside.
C. "3  DISTANT RIDGE": a small number of broad low-poly mountain silhouettes set farther back, lower contrast and cooler desaturated green-grey, asymmetrical slopes with saddle gaps but ground below, distinctly not stacked boxes, tiered cakes, a ring of pointy pyramids or huge repetitive peaks. Max distant peaks modestly taller than inner scenery, village remains focus.
Keep wide open meadow bands between plot backs and cliffs. Some sparse very simple flowers, faceted bushes, 2 natural recessed teal water ponds, one modest waterfall integrated into a cliff break. Actual water appearance, no blue flat plastic pads. Ground is continuous to the distance, softly fades into sky at far edge, don't present as a floating diorama island with exposed vertical outer cuts.
At both ends of straight road, one wide dark polygonal tunnel opening integrated into a single hillside with visible smooth road approach. Connected border continues over tunnel, keep roadway clear.
BOTTOM 35%: three separated informative larger close-up panels on cream:
"JOINED CORNER": clean low-poly wraparound rock cliff corner with one continuous grass cap and sloped foothill behind, show no overlapping slabs.
"TUNNEL TRANSITION": chunky low-poly hill arch with clear road aperture, broad grass slope over top, cliff joins at shoulders.
"LAYER PROFILE": clean side-view schematic from meadow left toward background right, showing low inner faceted cliff then broad rolling grassy slope then modest pale distant ridge. Labels "Meadow", "Cliff", "Slopes", "Ridge" aligned with features. Continuous ground below all. This profile must be spatially consistent with hero image.
Overall polished but genuinely simple, few faces, restrained details, practical modular geometry. No user interface, no currency, no HUD, no confetti. This is a proposed border replacement concept, not finished game screenshot.
