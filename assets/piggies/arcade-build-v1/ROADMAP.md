# Arcade collection roadmap

The current collection contains **10 imported piggies**, validated in Roblox Studio. All uploads are approved; publishing the live place remains separate.

| Tier | Built | Still proposed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | None yet | Player Two, Button Mash, Joystick, Bubble Screen, High Score, Bonus Round, Game Over |
| Rare | Player One, Retro Carpet, Pixel, Circuit Board | Checkerboard, 8-Bit Sunset, Maze Runner, Token, Pinball |
| Epic | Respawn, Power-Up, Synthwave | Combo, CRT |
| Legendary | Jackpot, Final Boss, Mecha Player | — |

The original proposal remains 24 total, with Player One moved to Rare after its selected 16-bit authored coat. Target distribution is therefore seven Common, nine Rare, five Epic and three Legendary. Counts are content planning, not changes to crate odds or runtime rarity.

Common concepts still need to establish a recognizable arcade identity through simple colors, existing part treatments and expressions. Do not quietly give them detailed Rare coats or glowing effects just to make the theme visible.

Final Boss uses angular plum armor, hinged guard motion and rear energy blocks. Mecha Player uses steel boots, navy guards and rear thrusters. Both preserve the base pig; neither replaces it with a humanoid robot.
